- FerroSMART reads a patient's own record during a standalone launch as a SMART Backend Services client (SMART App Launch 2.2.0, Backend Services): its own confidential client, registered from the new `[ferrosmart.backend]` table, obtains a `system/Patient.rs` client credentials token with a `private_key_jwt` assertion through its own token endpoint, so the token's `sub` is its `client_id` (IHE IUA §3.71.4.2.2.1) and the acquisition is the endpoint's `IHE.IUA.71` record. The same client reads the Patient of the paired-EHR lookup and of the token exchange, a wallet login's identifier search and the PIXm or PDQm patient index. A resource server that exchanges every user token onward reads these with its own credential. Each read is narrowed to the one matched `Patient`, one token per read, revoked once answered, and its access record is filed under the patient on whose behalf FerroSMART read; both are our own design (GDPR Art 5(1)(c), Art 25; Regulation (EU) 2025/327 Annex II 3.2(b) is silent on system access).
- `[ferrosmart.backend]` (`client_id`, `algorithm` `ES384` or `RS384`, and the `[ferrosmart.backend.keys]` ring, which shares no key with `[ferrosmart.keys]`) is required when people log in at a FHIR base. A configuration without it is refused at start.
- The picker's searches and reads, a professional's EHR lookups and the `fhirUser` lookup run under a grant of the signed-in person to FerroSMART's own client, revoked with its token once the request is answered, so a resource server that exchanges the token onward finds the person's own grant.
- The token exchange takes a professional's own token too (`user/` scopes alone, a `fhirUser` that names no `Patient`) and issues a token for the paired openEHR base with `user/composition-*.r` and `user/aql-*.s` at most, naming the professional and no patient.
