- Breaking: the sealed session store is removed. The memory store is the only store: the session, its refresh token, its tokens for other resource servers and its justifications stay in the console's process and never reach the browser. A console runs as one instance, or routes each session to one replica at its ingress (sticky sessions), and a restart signs every operator out. For this release `[session] store = "sealed"` is refused at load with `ConfigError::SealedRemoved`, which names `memory`, and `key` and `key_file` with `ConfigError::SealingKeyRemoved`; the next release deletes them. Gone with it: `config::KEY_BYTES`, the `ConfigError` variants `NoKey`, `KeyUnused`, `KeyBase64`, `KeyShort` and `OfflineAccessSealed`, and the `SessionError` variants `Seal`, `Unsealed` and `TooLarge`.
