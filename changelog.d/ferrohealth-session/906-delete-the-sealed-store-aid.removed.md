- Breaking: the removal aid of the sealed session store is deleted, one release after the store. `StoreKind::Sealed`, the `key` and `key_file` fields of `SessionSection`, and the `ConfigError` variants `SealedRemoved` and `SealingKeyRemoved` are gone, so `[session] store = "sealed"` is an unknown value and `key` and `key_file` are unknown keys, refused when the configuration reads.
